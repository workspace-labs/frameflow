# Story Time

- kind: tablet
- about: A reading app for children, on a tablet.
- version: 1
- date: 2026-10-06

## Reading a book
From the shelf to the last page.

1. Shelf [grid]: Sees the books with big covers, taps one. → 2
2. Book [detail]: Sees the cover and the title, taps "Read". → 3
3. Reading [message]: Swipes through the pages, with pictures. → 4
4. The end [done]: Sees a star and "Well done!", taps "Back to the shelf". → 1
