## Finishing a task
From opening the app to a task marked done.

1. Sign in [form]: Sees the sign-in form. Types the work email and password, clicks "Sign in". → 2
   ! Account locked: Sees "Ask your manager to unlock it". → end
2. My tasks [list]: Sees today's tasks. Clicks one. → 3
3. Task [detail]: Sees the details and the notes. Clicks "Mark done". → 4
4. Done [done]: Sees the task move to "Finished today". Clicks "Back to my tasks". → 2
5. Weekly report [message]: Sees the week's finished tasks, opened from the menu. → end
