## Booking a visit
From the home page to a confirmed appointment.

1. Home [grid]: Sees the clinic's services. Clicks "Book a visit". → 2
2. Choose a doctor [list]: Sees the doctors by name and speciality. Picks one. → 3
3. Pick a time [form]: Sees the free days and times. Chooses one, clicks "Continue". → 4
   ! Time just taken: Sees "That time was just booked". Picks another. → 3
4. Your details [form]: Sees the form. Types name and phone number, clicks "Book". → 5
5. Booked [done]: Sees the date, the doctor and a reminder note.

## Finding the clinic
From any page to the map and opening hours.

1. Contact [detail]: Sees the address, the map and the opening hours. → end
